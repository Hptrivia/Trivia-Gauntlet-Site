🎮 10 Ways to Play

  • Marathon: 30 questions per round — the ultimate test of how well you really know a theme.
  • Challenge: Quick 10-question rounds, perfect for a fast play on the go.
  • Survival: Sudden death — one wrong answer and it's over. How long can you last?
  • Mashup: Combine up to 5 themes into one custom quiz.
  • Episode Quizzes: Deep-dive trivia for specific episodes of select shows.
  • Versus: Local multiplayer — pass and play with 2–4 friends, best of 3, 5 or 10.
  • Daily Trivia: A fresh 10-question mix across themes, every day.
  • Daily Wordle: One themed 5-letter word a day, 6 guesses.
  • Word Search: Themed word-search puzzles to hunt and solve.
  • Wordle: Endless themed word-guessing on your favorite franchises.
